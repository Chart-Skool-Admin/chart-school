Why it sticks

Built to beat the forgetting curve.

Reading feels like learning. Remembering under pressure is what counts. Chart School is designed around the learning science of retrieval, spacing and mixing it up.

01 · RetrievalRecall before you read

Every session opens with questions, not notes. Pulling an idea out of memory is what makes it last.

02 · SpacingMistakes come back

Questions you miss return in later sessions, and your weakest topic gets its own drill, until you get them right.

03 · InterleavingOld charts, mixed in

Earlier levels keep showing up between new ones, so you learn to tell setups apart, not just recognise them.
